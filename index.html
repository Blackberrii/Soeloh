The project

HorizonsRP checking…

A Garry's Mod DarkRP server, built and tuned from the ground up.

This is where the actual hours go. Not a side hustle, not a hosting business — just a DarkRP server I keep making better: tuning the economy, writing custom jobs, scripting with E2/Wiremod, and building out systems players actually notice.
